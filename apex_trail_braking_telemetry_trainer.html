How to Modulate Brake & Gas: Early vs. Geometric vs. Late Apex

Phase 1: Before Turn-In
100% Threshold Braking

Hit the brake hardest in a straight line (between the 120m and 70m boards). 100% of the tire's traction budget is used for longitudinal deceleration.

Phase 2: Turn-In to Apex
Trail Braking (Bleed Off)

As you turn the wheel toward the apex, progressively release the brake (e.g., 70% → 40% → 0%). Keeping slight brake pressure pins weight on the front tires so the car rotates without understeering!

Phase 3: At & After Apex
Unwind & Progressive Gas

Reach 0% brake at the Apex. As you pass the clipping point and unwind the steering wheel, squeeze the throttle smoothly (30% → 70% → 100%).

Why Apex Timing + Road Shape Dictate Your Pedals

LATE APEX

Best for Corner Exit & Decreasing Radius: You brake slightly deeper and turn in later, clipping the inside kerb past the midpoint of the corner. Essential for Decreasing Radius turns and corners preceding a Long Straight.

GEOMETRIC / INCREASING

High Mid-Corner Momentum: Works best in 45° Sweepers and Increasing Radius corners (like Monza's Parabolica) where the road naturally opens up on exit, letting you roll onto 100% gas early.

DOUBLE APEX

Two Clipping Points (A1 & A2): Clip the first apex (A1), let the car drift mid-track while breathing on the throttle/trail-brake to rotate, then clip the second exit apex (A2) for full throttle launch!

EARLY APEX TRAP

The Novice Mistake: Turning in too early clips the kerb before mid-corner. The corner radius tightens after the apex, forcing you to slam the brakes or lift off the gas at corner exit to avoid understeering off-track!
